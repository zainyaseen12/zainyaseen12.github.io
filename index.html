FAQs
What is your project process like?

I start by understanding your goals, requirements, and target audience. Then I plan the structure, build the website, test everything carefully, and optimize it before launch.

How long does a website project take?

The timeline depends on the project size and complexity. A standard business website usually takes around 1–3 weeks, while larger eCommerce or custom development projects may require more time.

What services do you offer?

I provide WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, Wix and Squarespace development, Figma-to-website conversion, custom functionality, API integrations, speed optimization, technical SEO, website security, and ongoing maintenance.

Can you work with my existing website or design?

Yes. I can redesign, improve, repair, optimize, or add new functionality to an existing website. I can also convert your Figma or reference design into a fully responsive and functional website.

Do you provide support after the website is launched?

Yes. I provide post-launch support for fixes, updates, performance improvements, backups, security, maintenance, and future website enhancements whenever needed.
